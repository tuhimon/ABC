Our Purpose

Empowering People to Lead Healthier Lives

At Aditya Birla Health Insurance, our purpose goes beyond providing health insurance. We aim to be a trusted partner in our customers’ health journeys, helping them take proactive steps towards better health and well-being.

Through our wellness ecosystem, digital health initiatives and personalised support, we encourage people to understand their health, prevent illness, adopt healthier habits and make positive lifestyle changes.

02 · Journey & Targets

First 90 days roadmap with target clarity

A phased growth trajectory with clear focus areas, progressive target expectations, and dedicated coaching at every milestone.

Step 1 · Day 1 Onwards

Induction & Training

• 3 Days Physical Training: In-person classroom induction covering Aditya Birla Group heritage, core health products, advisor ecosystem & system onboarding.

• Level 2 (After 1 Week): 2 days advanced interactive training conducted live on MS Teams.

Focus & Output: 100% attendance, product certification & portal login activation.
Step 2 · Within 10 Days

Business Activation (T2P)

Time to Productivity (T2P): Activate your business as quickly as possible within the first 10 days of joining with joint field visits and active guidance from your Reporting Manager & Buddy.

Target Clarity (T2P): ₹20,000 target business to be achieved & activated within your first 10 days.
Step 3 · M0 → M3 (90 Days)

Get Set Grow (GSG)

The 90-day milestone spanning from M0 (joining month) through M3 (onwards). Fulfill either of the conditions below to qualify for GSG:

GSG Qualification (Either / Or):
I. Recruit 7 Agency QR (Quality Recruitment) advisors
— OR —
II. Achieve 70% of 3-Month Target
03 · Role Clarity

Your role as a Field Sales Professional

Bringing health security and proactive wellness to families while building strong, long-lasting partner and advisor relationships.

Field Sales Role

Protecting Lives, Empowering Health

As a Field Sales professional at Aditya Birla Health Insurance, you are the face of ABHI in the community. You consult, educate, and advise families on securing their health and financial well-being while bringing our Purpose to life every day.

🚗
Territory Outreach & Customer Meetings

Travel across your designated territory to meet new families, understand their unique healthcare priorities, and service existing policyholders with prompt care.

💡
Need-Based Solution Advisory

Understand individual customer requirements and recommend tailored, suitable health insurance solutions and riders at their time and place of convenience.

🌱
Champion Proactive Wellness & HealthReturns™

Educate clients on Digital Health Assessments (DHA), Well-Being Scores, and motivate daily fitness tracking through Active Dayz™ to earn up to 100% HealthReturns™.

🤝
Build Strong Partner & Advisor Relationships

Recruit, onboard, train, and maintain strong, mutually rewarding relationships with agents, advisors, distributors, and channel partners.

🎯
Achieve Monthly Business & Growth Milestones

Proactively manage your daily pipeline, conduct customer reviews, and consistently achieve Time-to-Productivity (T2P) and Get Set Grow (GSG) targets.

The ABHI Field Edge
